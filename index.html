Privacy policy

Walingo does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

The app works entirely offline. It does not request any device permissions and does not connect to the internet.

Your notes, favorites, learning progress and settings are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time from Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

The app contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking.

If you have questions, contact the developer through the app's Google Play listing.
